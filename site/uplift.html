Read alongside · Anthropic, 6 Jul 2026

They found the workspace inside. We put it outside.

This week Anthropic published "Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models." A new lens on Claude's activations found a small, privileged internal workspace — a few percent of the representation — that the model reasons with: holding "Mars" before it answers, flagging a prompt injection, noticing a bug in code (their own example). Suppress that workspace and shallow classification survives, but multi-hop reasoning, translation, and composition collapse below a small model. The one thing robust to suppression is reasoning the model has written down — chain-of-thought — because then the page becomes the workspace.

Their finding · from the inside

  • The operational reasoning lives in a small workspace — bug-noticing included.
  • It drives exactly the tasks that separate tiers: inference, composition, translation.
  • Externalize it onto the page and it survives ablation — so it's portable in principle.
  • But it stays locked in the weights, read only through their lens, rented per token.

Our result · from the outside

  • We externalize that workspace as a written checklist — the frontier's move, on the page.
  • A cheap model running it matches the frontier on exactly those tasks.
  • The artifact is portable, auditable, and yours — any provider, run local.
  • A better use for the space: take the workspace out of the weights and own it.

Their science and our engineering are one claim from two sides: the part of a frontier model that does the operational work is small, separable, and — once it is on the page — cheap to run anywhere. AXM Genesis extends the same move to knowledge itself — compile it into a signed artifact and take the stochastic model off the critical path.

What we did — and what it showed

Seven tasks, real model instances (haiku, sonnet, opus), graded blind by deterministic checkers. On checkable tasks the cheap model already ties the frontier. Where a gap appeared, a small harness — decompose the work into focused passes, union the findings — closed it and then crossed it.
