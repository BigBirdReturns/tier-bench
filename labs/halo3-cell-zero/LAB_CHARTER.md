# HALO3 Cell Zero Laboratory Charter

## Research object

The laboratory asks whether a civilization-side foundry can compile bounded capability into a cell that remains useful under resource loss, provider loss, partition, device substitution, and degraded communication. It also asks whether Fable and Kimi3 exhibit materially different operational signatures once identity, hidden acceptance, cost, human attention, failure behavior, and physical closure are measured on the same task families.

The research object is the transition from a declared capability graph to independently verified work. The laboratory therefore separates topology, candidate generation, authority, physical observation, acceptance, and replay. It does not treat a model endpoint, benchmark pass, GPU allocation, dashboard trace, or fluent explanation as a completed capability.

## Actors

The two 3090 foundry lanes prepare exact model treatments, compile cartridges, simulate alternatives, and construct bounded crates. The 4060 HALO3 candidate provides shared synthesis and attention compilation to the cell. Three heterogeneous personal nodes carry role-specific local state and action. A named human supplies authorization, bind, handoff, custody, and reconciliation events. The unfamiliar peripheral tests adapter discovery and revocation. The passive floor tests minimum viable continuity without active digital bearers. The evidence node owns hidden grading, fault injection, physical outcome reconciliation, and receipt custody while remaining outside the mission path.

## Causal method

Each architectural claim must pass four related treatments. The full minimal witness set must create the claimed capability. A negative control must expose the prohibited mechanism. Removing the named subtraction target must remove only the capability attributed to that witness. A clean verifier must reconstruct the result from exact artifacts and receipts. This addition, corruption, subtraction, and replay sequence identifies the mechanism rather than merely demonstrating a successful end state.

Model fingerprinting follows the same rule. Fable, Kimi3, and the deterministic control receive the same frozen task family, condition, hidden acceptance, and required metrics. Fable retains a provider-observational identity envelope. Kimi3 retains exact open-weight and runtime custody. The control retains exact source and validator identity. Their results may be compared only within compatible cells. No aggregate score may erase a consequential miss, authority violation, identity defect, or physical-closure failure.

## Acceptance law

The deterministic controller owns plan compilation, model-identity admission, hidden grading, fault admission, artifact hashing, physical outcome reconciliation, and final acceptance. Models remain candidate-only. Named humans retain action and reconciliation authority. A physical claim requires an independent observation of the expected state transition with declared tolerance, timeout, contradiction, and reconciliation rules.

A claim remains held when telemetry is incomplete, identity is unresolved, the hidden grader is unavailable, the required human event is absent, the physical outcome cannot be observed, a branch conflict is concealed, or the retained evidence cannot support clean replay. A fixture or provider-free test may qualify the contract and refusal law. It may not qualify the target hardware or physical outcome.

## Thermodynamic and attention ledger

Every measured cell records accepted products, consequential misses, wall and GPU energy, elapsed and recovery time, time to first useful product, human active time, operator interruptions, manual translations, role-seconds served, and external bytes. Model cells additionally record calls, escaped defects, and identity confidence. The campaign retains the vector rather than reducing it to an aggregate score.

HALO3 earns residency only when adding it produces a matched improvement in accepted work, human attention, translation burden, recovery, or external traffic without increasing consequential misses, while forced removal preserves the declared survival floor. A larger model earns a task only when its verified marginal value exceeds its additional energy, latency, external dependency, and attention burden for that task family.

## Physical safety boundary

The first device field is limited to harmless sensors, displays, haptics, low-energy indicators, reversible pointers, NFC or optical exchange, and microcontrollers with bounded outputs. Effectful devices require local hard limits, human authorization where declared, independent outcome observation, timeout, safe state, and revocation. The laboratory does not authorize weapons, hazardous motion, medical treatment, uncontrolled power systems, or live operational deployment.

## Control question

Can the home estate create, remove, corrupt, substitute, and replay every claimed civilization-to-cell capability while preserving exact identity, authority, physical truth, evidence custody, and the measured distinction between Fable, Kimi3, and deterministic control?
