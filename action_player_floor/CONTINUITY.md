# Action Player Floor continuity

The floor must remain useful after every current repository, engine package, provider,
maintainer, and model has changed.

## Stable mission

Preserve one authoritative action chain while allowing presentation, input, camera,
animation, effects, audio, haptics, HUD, assets, engines, and providers to evolve.

The floor may validate, measure, compare, inspect, challenge, preserve, and report.
It may not create gameplay law, choose a permanent supplier, authenticate a mandate,
issue a human receipt, or accept a product.

## What must survive a rewrite

If every line of Python is replaced, preserve:

```text
axm-action-player-floor-catalog/1
axm-action-player-intent/1
axm-action-player-change/1
axm-action-player-negative-witness/1
axm-action-player-floor-report/1
```

Preserve canonical content identities, integer-only semantic JSON, duplicate-key
refusal, organ ownership, evidence tiers, negative witnesses, rollback records, and
the prohibition on aggregate readiness scores.

## Recovery order

1. Read `START_HERE.md`.
2. Validate `catalog.json`.
3. Validate the worked UNDERDRAIN intent.
4. Validate every negative witness.
5. Validate every change proposal.
6. Rebuild `floor-report.json` and require byte equality.
7. Verify that Arc and World coordination issues still exist or record successors.
8. Verify that no accepted product fact has been inferred from the floor report.

## Migration law

A format change requires:

- a new format version;
- before and after fixtures;
- a deterministic migration tool;
- preserved and changed fields;
- an explicit failure default;
- a rollback point;
- at least one negative witness for the new ambiguity;
- proof that old records remain readable or a declared refusal.

A renamed project, provider, engine, or maintainer does not change an organ's
authority. A fork creates a new implementation identity. A provider replacement does
not change player intent. A new receiver does not acquire Arc law. A new evidence
system does not acquire acceptance.

## Updating ownership

Ownership changes only when the constitutional responsibility changes. Moving files
between repositories is not an ownership change. If a future estate combines
repositories, preserve separate organ records and authority membranes.

## Updating gates

A gate may be added when a demonstrated failure is not represented. It requires:

- owner;
- evidence tier;
- hard or advisory status;
- description;
- failure default;
- positive fixture;
- negative witness.

A gate may be removed only through a recorded migration proving that another gate
fully contains its failure class. Historical failures remain in the record.

## Updating commodities

A provider enters as a candidate behind a capability cell. It requires an exact
version, license, adapter, shared fixture, independent verifier, budgets, offline or
declared network behavior, fallback, substitution test, and rip-out receipt.

A provider can disappear without making the player intent, accepted trace, evidence,
or fallback unreadable.

## Succession receipt

Before leaving the project, record:

```text
current catalog and report IDs
current worked intent IDs
open coordination records
qualified provider cells
open gates
accepted product facts, if any
negative witnesses
latest reconstruction command
known evidence limits
```

Never summarize open engine, human, or acceptance gates as a percentage.

The control question is whether a successor can delete every current presentation
provider and still reconstruct what the game means, what evidence exists, what failed,
who may decide, and what must be rebuilt.
