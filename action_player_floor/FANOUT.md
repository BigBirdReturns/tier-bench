## Adoption rule

Each lane must cite the exact floor commit and record IDs. A lane may extend its own
owned schema or implementation, but it may not silently alter another organ's owner,
authority, evidence tier, negative witness, or acceptance boundary.

A completed lane returns:

```text
exact source ref
owned change record
positive fixtures
negative witnesses
qualification venues
artifacts and digests
open gates
rollback point
explicit non-claims
```

The floor report remains `productAccepted: false` until all lower evidence tiers pass
and a named authority acts under an explicit mandate.
