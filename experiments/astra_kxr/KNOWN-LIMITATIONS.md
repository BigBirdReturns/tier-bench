# ASTRA-KXR-1 — Known limitations (frozen with stage 1)

1. **Black-box non-identifiability.** External observables cannot uniquely
   determine internal architecture. Every terminal is "-CONSISTENT", never
   proof. The named failure mode of this campaign is promoting a suggestive
   latency surface into an internal-architecture claim.

2. **Reporting-truthfulness dependency.** The recurrent-depth terminal is
   conditional on truthful provider reasoning-token accounting. If that
   accounting is absent or degraded, recurrent-depth and hidden token-serial
   implementations are observationally equivalent here, by construction.

3. **Serving confounds.** Batching, autoscaling, speculative decoding,
   routing, queueing, and time-of-day load all shape latency. Blocked
   randomization, route-stability gates, transport baselines, and
   invalidated-cell reporting mitigate but do not remove them.

4. **Statistical power.** Seventy-two subject calls at four replicates per
   cell support only a provisional classification. A single mid-campaign
   routing change can invalidate a large fraction of the lattice with no
   budget to recover. The confirmatory campaign, not the sentinel, carries
   any architectural conclusion.

5. **Classifier transfer.** Calibration signatures come from local hardware
   and local serving. Only normalized shape invariants transfer; any use of
   absolute local timings against provider telemetry is invalid.

6. **Single-source premise.** The recurrent-depth attribution for the
   subject rests on one paywalled report. Publicly corroborated wording
   supports only "a technique that reveals less of the model's thinking."

7. **Provider-terms exposure.** Publicly preregistering an
   architecture-inference campaign may be read against provider terms
   restricting discovery of underlying components. Account interruption
   mid-campaign is a real failure mode; the campaign account must be one
   whose loss is acceptable, and this exposure is disclosed rather than
   worked around.

8. **Chronology limits.** The instrument and doctrine predate the subject's
   public existence (July 2026, remote custody); the observatory and its
   `astral-candidate` placeholder do not (2026-09-01/02, after public
   naming). This preregistration claims prediction standing only from its
   own freeze commits, and only if they precede public callability.

9. **Conflicted authorship.** Drafted by a resident frontier lineage that is
   both the observatory's first subject and a commercial rival of the
   campaign subject. Mitigation is structural (machine-derived closure,
   gated graders, receipt-only conclusions), not narrative.
