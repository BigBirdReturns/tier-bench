## 1. Why this supersession exists

ASTRA-KXR-1 (commit `ea451a9e6894c10b09666606d4a445d8cc2826e4`) stated
waterline-before-geometry as its governing order in section 2, then budgeted
an unconditional 72-call K×R lattice sentinel in section 5. Those two
sections contradict each other: the sentinel spends the subject ceiling on
architecture geometry regardless of waterline outcome, which is the exact
inversion the refinery doctrine prohibits. Under ASTRA-KXR-1's own amendment
rules, correcting campaign design requires a new preregistration id. This is
that correction. The ea451a9 freeze remains in custody as a receipt of what
was frozen and when; nothing is deleted or rewritten.

## 2. Decision authority — the waterline runtime

The model waterline holds primary decision and spend authority. Per cell:

```text
identical cell
  -> cheapest admitted model + existing harness, hidden machine grade
  -> PASS: stop; subject spend = 0
  -> FAIL: classify the exact wall
      -> one next justified rung
          -> PASS: stop
          -> surviving boundary:
              one subject observation
                -> capture K x R sidecar telemetry
                -> isolate judgment residue
                -> compute marginal shadow price
                -> compile distillation work order
                -> encode verifier / schema / search / control
                -> rerun the cheap floor
```

Nested recurrence is owned by H3: the first comparison at any surviving
boundary is whether the existing cheap harness converts that boundary into
search. Only a boundary that survives cheap search, deterministic
computation, and explicit framing justifies testing additional external
recurrence, as a residue-local diagnostic — never as a formation benchmark
suite priced in subject calls.

## 3. Execution law

- The 80-request ceiling is a containment boundary, not a target.
- One attempt per justified rung; no retries inside measurement cells.
- No subject call re-confirms a cleared floor (LESSONS rule 2).
- No full homogeneous or heterogeneous formation matrix.
- No model judges its own necessity; verdicts issue from hidden graders and
  machine-derived closure only.

## 4. Geometry instrumentation

On every waterline-justified subject call, the observatory retains the full
ASTRA-KXR-1 observable vector (timing through SSE granularity, reported
reasoning tokens, route identity, accuracy, and recurrence-sensitive
perturbations where the cell permits). These sidecar observations feed the
four frozen analyses and may support any of the six terminals.

Stated consequence, frozen now: sidecar-only telemetry lacks controlled K×R
structure, so all four architecture-positive terminals may be unreachable
and INCONCLUSIVE by construction is an acceptable and publishable outcome.

Optional gated instrumentation block: a synthetic-lattice telemetry block of
at most 24 subject calls, pure instrumentation with no capability claim, MAY
be executed only under an explicit separate operator authorization recorded
as its own gated act before execution. It is disabled by default, draws no
justification from the waterline, and its absence never blocks campaign
closure. If never authorized, the geometry question closes on sidecar
evidence alone.

## 5. Native outputs

1. Waterline table: each exact cell, cheapest passing route, first failing
   route, first passing route, evidence coordinate.
2. Residue map: the smallest rule boundary or missing judgment explaining

3. Shadow-price ledger: marginal cost of each uniquely cleared cell.
4. Distillation work orders: residue converted into schemas, deterministic
   checks, search procedures, decomposition rules, or independent
   validators, then the cheap floor rerun.
5. K×R sidecar report under the frozen analyses and terminals.
6. Machine-derived qualification and closure receipts from the observatory.
