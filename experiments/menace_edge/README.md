# MENACE-EDGE-01

This directory contains two related but distinct instruments.

The **seam census** mines sanitized donor piles and their intersections to identify the invariants that recur across local control, readiness, people and communications, platform sovereignty, evidence and qualification, and durable orchestration. It compiles a strict seam catalog, negative-witness catalog, coverage matrix, exact cost-weighted minimal witness plan, and deterministic Venn census report.

The **qualification campaign** prospectively freezes workload shapes, roles, stream families, connectivity profiles, hardware profiles, treatments, fault injections, metrics, and acceptance law for later measured execution.

## Committed products

```text
donor_piles.json
  6 donor piles and 18 sanitized donor records

seam_catalog.json
  18 mandatory seams and 18 mandatory negative witnesses

coverage_matrix.json
  8 candidate integrated witnesses

minimal_witness_plan.json
  exact five-witness minimum covering every mandatory seam and negative witness

seam_census_report.json
SEAM_CENSUS.md
  deterministic pile, seam, Venn-intersection, support, and gap report

menace_edge_01.json
  315-cell prospective physical and operational qualification campaign
```

The exact minimal witness set is:

```text
witness.cooperative-handoff
witness.multi-role-handoff
witness.partitioned-controller
witness.physical-availability
witness.stack-recovery
```

This is a requirements-mining result rather than field acceptance. The declared cost units rank candidate witness combinations inside the frozen set-cover problem. They are not dollars, engineering-hour estimates, or an aggregate readiness score.

## Privacy and evidence boundary

The public donor ledger contains no private source bytes, personal names, customer records, operational coordinates, model weights, or hardware measurements. Private and mixed donors contribute only sanitized workload shapes and evidence classes. Exact private evidence remains outside the repository under separate custody.

The derived products are deterministic. CI regenerates the minimal witness plan, census JSON, and Markdown report and requires byte equality with the committed copies. Any change to a donor pile, seam, negative witness, coverage relation, or selection constraint creates new content identities.
