Her reflection's hand arrived at her cheek a half-second after her fingers touched it, as if the glass between them had widened into time.
In the morning mirror, she watched her other self move like someone listening to a song that had already finished.
The lag isn't in the glass—it's in the space between them now, and she feels it widen with every motion.
When she stopped moving, the reflection kept going for one suspended breath, and she understood she was watching her own ghost learn to live without her.
