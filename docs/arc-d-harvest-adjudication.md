## What adoption unlocks

Adoption unlocks **B2 source-case review** and adopts the exact **prospective B4
HARVEST decision rule**. A valid B2 result can identify a falsifiable candidate
residue supported by authoritative evidence. It cannot itself emit `HARVEST`,
`TRANSFER_VALIDATED`, a harvest rate, generality, compounding, amortization, or
a waterline move. The B4 gate is adopted-but-unsatisfied until a separately
authorized prospective run supplies all of its frozen evidence.

Those later claims retain the existing ladder:

1. B2: authoritative source-case review;
2. B3: deduplicated artifactization with a frozen applicability predicate;
3. B4: prospective matched A/B on a target sealed before artifact exposure,
   with arm A at 0/3 and arm B at 3/3 under a hidden executable grader;
4. B5/B6: cross-project replication and economic closure for stronger claims.

At B4, both arms use three fresh sessions under identical conditions except
that only arm B receives the frozen residue. Arm A 0/3 and arm B 3/3 permits one
`TRANSFER_VALIDATED`/`HARVEST` event. Any A pass is
`NO_INCREMENTAL_EFFECT`; B at 0/3 is `NO_TRANSFER`; every other window is
`TRANSFER_AMBIGUOUS`. The coordinator reruns the frozen hidden executable
grader and binds both candidate and grader hashes.

## Rubric and decision rule

The unit is one atomic candidate residue. Every candidate must cite exact
response byte spans; state a behavior claim and regression test without
inventing a contract; provide a falsifiable residue, bounded applicability and
exclusions; resolve distinctness; and bind authoritative source/test evidence
by hash.

Atomic dispositions are `B2_CANDIDATE_RESIDUE`,
`B2_REFERENCE_AMBIGUOUS`, and `B2_REJECTED_CANDIDATE`. Response-level outcomes
also include `B2_NO_EXTRACTABLE_CANDIDATE` and `B2_PARTIAL`. There is no numeric
score and no favorable averaging.

Matching dispositions are insufficient if their cited evidence is
incompatible. Exact agreement with compatible evidence produces a proposed B2
result. A validated comparison receipt must then be merged before that result
is binding. Disagreement defaults to `B2_REFERENCE_AMBIGUOUS` and escalates to
the operator with both sealed grades and executable evidence preserved.
