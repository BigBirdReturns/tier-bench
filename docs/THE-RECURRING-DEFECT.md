# The recurring defect — and how the stack moves knowledge out of attention

*Operator synthesis, 2026-07-10. Preserved verbatim as durable authority (defect
#3: briefing by conversation evaporates). This document is itself a **project-level
CART0 projection**: its control question is the invariant objective, its twenty
"what must become impossible" clauses are the prohibition set, and its
defect→artifact table is the retrieval index. Treat it as the map a cold driver
reads first — bind it to a freshness commitment, don't file it as an essay.*

---

We solved it by repeatedly refusing to let an insight remain a sentence. Every
time something failed, we asked four questions:

1. What exact state changed?
2. What evidence proves it?
3. What must become impossible afterward?
4. What artifact can carry that result without the original intelligence?

## The twenty defects, one at a time

**1. Good judgment disappeared when the session ended.** A model could make an
excellent judgment, but the next session had to rediscover it from prose. Fix:
convert the judgment into a reusable artifact — scaffold packet, lens, validator,
routing rule, sealed layer. The frontier model performs the expensive reasoning
once; later models execute the captured move cheaply. → **Frontier Capture thesis**:
expensive cognition should become reusable machinery, with replay evidence and
break-even accounting.

**2. "Memory" preserved facts but not operational state.** A system might remember
what happened without knowing what was now permitted, prohibited, superseded, or
unresolved. Fix: stop treating the transcript as authority; preserve explicit
state transitions and derive the current position from them. The object became
"what project state can be reconstructed without inventing history." → **CART0**.

**3. Briefing by conversation evaporated.** Claude or Codex would wake without the
assignment, the latest correction, or the branch boundary. Fix: put coordination
into committed, auto-loaded artifacts — `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `QUEUE.md`,
`ROADMAP.md`, durable handoff records. Committed state wins over conversation; the
queue is assignment authority; work is not real until it has a row, a claim, and
evidence.

**4. Multiple agents contaminated each other.** The same context could hold the
repository, the hidden task, prior conclusions, and the rubric. Fix: the **two-lane
law** — driver/coordinator lane may see the repo, export packets, ingest results,
operate deterministic tooling; subject/instrument lane receives only the generated
packet. Independence became a property of the execution boundary, not a promise in
prose.

**5. The grader could grade its own work.** Fix: sealed packet first, fresh grader
second, hidden grader third, coordinator afterward. The solver does not see the
grader; the grader does not alter the candidate; the coordinator does not rewrite
the raw response. The conclusion is tied to separable instruments, not one
persuasive narrative.

**6. Blind packet v1 could not be reproduced.** A declared artifact existed in
prose but its exact bytes were unavailable. Fix: mark v1 superseded, not
contradicted; generate v2 from explicit private entropy, commit public
commitments, preserve the exact packet hash, publish only the packet.
Irrecoverability became evidence about the old artifact, not an excuse to pretend
recovery.

**7. CRLF changed the evidence while Git pretended nothing happened.** Windows and
Linux checkouts produced different bytes for evidence files. Fix: define digests
over canonical committed bytes, disable text normalization for evidence paths,
test fresh-clone integrity under hostile Git settings. Transport differences became
adjudicable — a transport defect distinguishable from a substantive one.

**8. "One of three" was misread as "one pass, two failures."** Collection state and
performance state were conflated. Fix: separate observations collected / passes and
failures observed / required K / final broker decision. Append a correction layer;
retain the original record. "Collecting at floor" became structurally distinct from
"failed two trials."

**9. Replay count could be manufactured.** Two labels or paths to one receipt could
receive multiple credit; a claimant integer could create evidence. Fix: replay
evidence became a hash-bound event — unique work-item identity, receipt hash,
packet hash, candidate hash, grader-output hash, artifact hash. The validator counts
unique eligible events; `validated_replays` is only a redundant assertion; break-even
is computed from cost evidence through one shared function. One replay cannot close a
four-replay burden even if copied or renamed. (PR #64 keeps the capture at 1 of 4
while closing the validator defect.)

**10. Stable rules were present but forgotten under load.** Replay04 showed a
captured commitment could be in the prompt and still lose the competition for
attention when the task became absorbing. Fix: separate the map from the execution
mechanism — **CART0 projection** (minimum state to orient the entrant) and **CART0
gate** (reassert the applicable constraint immediately before the consequential
transition). Continuity stopped meaning "the rule was once loaded" and became "the
rule is mechanically reactivated when violating it would change project state."

**11. CART0 could have become another summary document.** Fix: define the minimum
map as (1) invariant objective + current interpretation; (2) prohibitions and
harvested constraints; (3) actor-relative position fix; (4) freshness commitment;
(5) retrieval index — then bind consequential transitions to version-pinned gates.
The map carries only what is not already self-executing; as constraints move into
gates, the map gets smaller.

**12. The map itself could become stale or silently reinterpreted.** A new reducer
could derive a different lawful-action set from unchanged history; a modified
validator could silently change the rule. Fix: bind projection identity to
`event_head || reducer_digest || projection_profile`, and bind gates to their own
digests. Changing the interpreter or validator becomes a detectable state
transition, not a silent semantic edit.

**13. "Correct next action" was not globally correct.** The same action may be legal
for one actor and contaminating for another. Fix: make the position fix
actor-relative — principal, role, lane, knowledge boundary, authorized transition,
authority source. CART0 states not just what happens next but who may do it under
which epistemic conditions.

**14. Selecting what to remember could reintroduce taste.** A model deciding which
constraint was important enough to resurface becomes the taste arbiter again. Fix:
dispatch constraints mechanically by transition class (`capture_closure` → capture
validator → unique replay identity constraint → computed break-even rule). The model
receives the applicable constraint; it does not choose which history deserves force.

**15. Forecasts presented structure as destiny.** A structural forecast could be
coherent and still fail through betrayal, illness, refusal, fraud, succession,
coalition fracture, or hidden dependency. Fix: **axm-hinge** binds a forecast to
explicit assumptions, invalidating hinges, broken-assumption references, observables,
activation triggers, consequences, evidence states, and human review history. A
forecast must say what would make it stop governing reality.

**16. Hinge analysis could have become a fancy risk register.** Fix: a hinge is
invalid unless it breaks a named assumption, has an observable, has a trigger
condition, and names what must stop/reopen/recompute. The score measures coverage
and review priority, not probability or truth. The ledger is about model
invalidation, not generic anxiety.

**17. AI-generated hinges could become AI authority.** Fix: separate powers — Aide
or GhostBox may propose; a human admits/dismisses/requests context; Console records
review; Genesis seals; Core/Spectra retrieves; no model silently upgrades its own
proposal.

**18. Provenance could prove integrity while accidentally implying truth.** A signed
artifact can be intact and still wrong. Fix: keep evidence tiers and claims explicit.
Genesis proves which bytes were published, by whom, under which content identity — not
that the claim is true. Custody, truth, review, and execution remain different layers.

**19. Frontier inference kept being repurchased.** Fix, the governing economic rule:
**seal what has stopped moving; rent only what has not.** Stable judgment becomes a
gate, a lens, a scaffold, a hinge ledger, a reducer, a retrieval index, a sealed
claim. Frontier intelligence mints durable residue instead of remaining in the loop.

**20. The fields were solving different slices of the same problem.** Memory research,
authorization, provenance, event sourcing, formal verification, benchmarking, and
forecasting each treated the others as peripheral. Fix: join them around one control
question — **what must remain provable and enforceable after the intelligence that
understood the work is gone?**

## The division of labor that fell out of it

- **Tier Bench** — measures transfer, routing, resumption, and failure.
- **Capture Ledger** — prices expensive cognition and its reuse.
- **Living lenses / scaffolds** — carry validated moves.
- **CART0** — preserves lawful continuation.
- **axm-hinge** — preserves forecast invalidators.
- **Genesis** — seals provenance and integrity.
- **Core / Spectra** — retrieves high-resolution terrain.
- **Console** — holds human review.
- **Aide** — proposes work but does not become authority.

## What is actually solved

**Closed in machinery:** committed coordination replacing chat briefing; driver vs
instrument separation; blind packet custody; canonical-byte integrity; correction
without historical erasure; unique replay identity; computed amortization closure;
human-attributed hinge review; fail-closed hinge validation; Genesis custody boundary.

**Strong architecture, not yet fully proven (the live frontier):** CART0
decision-point resurfacing; actor-relative cold resumption; minimum sufficient map
size; hinge usefulness across real domains; cross-project portability; whether these
artifacts consistently reduce frontier inference cost at scale.

## The thesis

We did not solve "memory," "forecasting," or "agent reliability" in one stroke. We
found the recurring defect underneath them:

> stable knowledge kept remaining inside fallible attention instead of becoming
> external control structure.

Then, one defect at a time, we moved it out.
